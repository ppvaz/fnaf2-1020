How it works

The routine. At every :X2 and :X7: cams down, mask flick for Golden Freddy, flash the hall for Foxy, cams up, flash CAM 10, CAM 04, CAM 07, then CAM 11 and wind until the next anchor.

Why it works. A camera flash freezes everyone in that room for 6.66 s, and all seven stallable animatronics have to pass through those three rooms. Flashes 5 s apart never let the stun lapse. Foxy, Golden Freddy and Balloon Boy cannot be stalled.

The stun bars are the game. Three bars, top of the screen, each draining from 6.66 s. If one hits zero while somebody is standing in that room, they move.

Light budget. 50 s of flashlight has to cover a 420 s night — 119 ms of light per second survived. The HUD shows your running rate; over budget turns red.

Balloon Boy. Third laugh: keep the cams down across every 5 s interval. Fourth laugh: flash all three cams and mask up, then react to his leaving bang. A full-length attack leaves about 0.7 s to un-mask, raise the cams and re-flash.

The camera map geometry is a reconstruction, not an extraction — use Settings → Calibrate to match your device. Simulation constants come from the community AI breakdowns; a few are calibrated rather than sourced and are marked as such in src/config.js. This is a practice tool, not the game.
