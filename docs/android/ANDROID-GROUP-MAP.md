# Event-sheet coverage map

Generated by `tools/dump/coverage.py` against the office frame. Last
regenerated **2026-08-26** after the sourcing pass that took state coverage
from 72% to 89% and the unread list from 87 groups to 37. Regenerate it
after any sourcing pass — the point is that the blind spots stay a list rather
than a feeling.

Every targeted lookup this project has done answered its own question and told
us nothing about what we had not looked at. This is the answer to "what else
could be missing": each group is classified by what it is able to change, and
cross-referenced against the group numbers cited anywhere in the repo.

A group only matters to the simulator if it can write a character's position
or alterable values, or one of the named gameplay counters. `presentation`
groups draw view frames, animations and sounds; `setup` groups write per-night
constants; `other` groups are Fusion bookkeeping. The **state** and **input**
rows are the ones where a mechanic can hide.

**`g782-786` run down 2026-08-26, and neither half changes the model.**

- `g782-784` zero Foxy's `AlterableValue3` on **night 1** at `time of the night`
  12, 1 and 2. They are **redundant**: `g872` already zeroes the same value on
  night 1 on the one-second event, i.e. continuously, and `g873-874` do the same
  for night 2 hours 12 and 1. The engine's `foxyDormant` -- night 1 entire, plus
  night 2 until 2 AM -- is the correct reading of g872-874, and g782-784 add
  nothing to it. Recorded because a partial read of only g782-784 looks like a
  contradiction of the engine, and this project nearly filed one: 14 groups
  write that alterable, and three of them are not the rule.
- `g785-786` are Withered Freddy's cams-up second counter (`AlterableValue25`):
  `g785` clears it whenever `viewing == 0`, `g786` adds to it once per second
  while `viewing > 0`. That is the entry-streak clock, held per character in the
  source where the engine holds it once globally as `camsUpSince`. Equivalent
  while a single streak decides the entry, which is the case today; if a second
  character ever needs an independent streak, this is the group pair that says
  so.

Two of these clusters have already been run down and were worth it:
`g875-881` is the `hall movement` latch that blocks Golden Freddy's hall
exposure for five seconds after anyone transits the hallway (now implemented),
and `g458-477` turned out to be inert — a per-character drain of the `C`
counter that nothing gates on except the Puppet's branch in g406/407.
